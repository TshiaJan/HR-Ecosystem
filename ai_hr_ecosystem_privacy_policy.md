## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **AI HR Ecosystem** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding user privacy and transparency while providing advanced human resources intelligence, workforce analytics, and talent management simulation tools.

By accessing or using AI HR Ecosystem, you consent to the data principles described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

AI HR Ecosystem is engineered as an offline-capable, client-side dashboard application. We operate under strict data-minimization practices:

- **No Real Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or government identification numbers.
- **No Mandatory Account Registration:** Users do not need to create an account, log in, or link enterprise credentials to access any feature of the Application.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track, collect, or store GPS coordinates or location history.
- **No Biometric Data:** We do not collect facial scans, voice samples, or fingerprints.
- **No Camera or Microphone Access:** The Application does not record audio, capture images, or access your camera.
- **No Device Contacts or Storage Scanning:** We do not read your contact list, photo albums, or personal files.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

All employee records, departmental performance stats, performance review schedules, talent pool filters, and user interface preferences are simulated and stored locally on your device via browser/device storage (`localStorage`):

- **Simulated Employee Records & Job Profiles**
- **Departmental Metrics, Retention Goals & Hiring Budgets**
- **Scheduled Performance Reviews & Evaluation Reminders**
- **Customized UI Themes, Tab Selection, and Display Settings**

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded or transmitted to external servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing application data in your device settings.

---

## 4. Simulated Enterprise HR Data

All workforce data, employee names, salary projections, attrition predictions, and talent scoring shown within the Application are simulated demonstration metrics created for management training, modeling, and productivity optimization. No actual corporate personnel data is exposed.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The core application operates completely offline. If distributed application packages incorporate standard developer libraries or advertising platforms (such as Google Play Services or AdMob), those services may process non-identifiable technical data:

- Device model, operating system version, and general system performance data.
- Anonymous Advertising Identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID), subject to your device settings.
- Crash logs and diagnostic data to ensure application reliability.

These third-party platforms are governed by their own independent privacy policies.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

AI HR Ecosystem is designed for professionals, managers, students, and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable). If you believe personal information from a minor has been collected, please contact us immediately for deletion.

---

## 7. Data Security

Because all configurations and simulation logs remain in your device's local sandboxed environment, security is governed by your device operating system's security model. We follow modern secure coding guidelines to prevent unauthorized code execution.
